# General

## Commit conventions: Conventional Commits, atomic, no co-author trailer
All commits must:
- Follow Conventional Commits: `type(scope): summary` (types: feat, fix, chore, build, docs, refactor, test, etc.), lowercase imperative summary, no trailing period. Scope is the affected area (e.g. `auth`, `accounts`, `deps`).
- Be atomic: each commit is one coherent logical change with a working tree at that point (split unrelated changes into separate commits rather than one large commit).
- Never include a `Co-Authored-By:` trailer, even when generated by an AI agent.
